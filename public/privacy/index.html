Privacy Policy

PredictCare.AI provides educational resources, workshops, diagnostics, and business workflow consulting for healthcare companies. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact information you choose to provide, such as name, email, organization, role, and meeting details.
  • Business context you submit through forms, scorecards, email, Calendly, or workshop registration.
  • Basic website analytics and technical information, such as pages visited, browser type, referring page, and approximate location, when analytics tools are enabled.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and schedule conversations.
  • To deliver workshops, diagnostics, resources, and consulting services.
  • To improve HealthFounderOS content, tools, and workflows.
  • To send relevant follow-up about resources or services you requested. You may ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

Healthcare and PHI

Do not submit protected health information, patient records, or patient-specific information through this website, email forms, scorecards, or scheduling tools. PredictCare.AI website resources are designed for business workflow education and commercial operations support, not clinical decision support, diagnosis, treatment, or patient-specific advice.

HIPAA and security posture

PredictCare.AI does not claim that this public website or general educational tools are HIPAA-compliant systems of record. If a paid engagement requires access to PHI or regulated healthcare data, that scope must be reviewed separately and appropriate agreements, security controls, vendor tools, and a Business Associate Agreement, if applicable, must be in place before any such data is shared.

Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. We may use trusted service providers for hosting, analytics, scheduling, email, documents, payments, or communications. Those providers process information according to their own terms and privacy practices.

Data retention and requests

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for business, legal, and operational purposes. To request access, correction, or deletion of information you provided, contact croom@predictcare.ai.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site, services, or legal requirements evolve. Material updates will be reflected on this page.
