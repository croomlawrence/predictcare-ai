Diagnostic areas

Choose the function where AI could create the most leverage first.

Each diagnostic focuses on one function or workflow family so the review stays practical, fast, and easy for the CEO to act on.

Marketing

Content workflow, campaign planning, audience segmentation, compliant messaging, creative production, and performance learning loops.

Sales

Lead qualification, follow-up, CRM hygiene, proposal support, account intelligence, handoffs, and sales management cadence.

Care delivery

Operational workflows, patient-facing education boundaries, team handoffs, task routing, and human-in-the-loop support. No clinical decision automation.

Customer service

Inquiry handling, FAQ workflows, escalation paths, response quality, service recovery, and customer insight capture.

Investor relations

Investor updates, diligence room organization, Q&A prep, pipeline reporting, narrative consistency, and follow-up cadence.

Clinical trial management

Trial operations workflows, site communications, document organization, status reporting, recruitment-support boundaries, and issue escalation.

Performance analytics & reporting

Executive dashboards, KPI definitions, weekly reporting rhythm, metric interpretation, and decision-ready summaries.

Human resources / ways of working

Meeting habits, onboarding, SOPs, internal knowledge, role clarity, AI usage norms, and team adoption patterns.

How it works

10 business days from kickoff to CEO readout.

The diagnostic is intentionally bounded. It is not a vague AI strategy project. It is a practical audit of one function with findings the CEO can act on.

1

Kickoff

60-minute CEO/function-lead session to define the function, business objective, constraints, systems, and current pain points.

2

Review

We review non-PHI workflows, artifacts, templates, reports, CRM/screenshots, SOPs, meeting notes, or process examples you approve.

3

Score

We score the function for workflow friction, AI leverage, data readiness, governance risk, adoption risk, and near-term ROI potential.

4

Readout

60-minute CEO readout with findings, quick wins, risk flags, recommended next steps, and the right path: implement internally, Pro Evolve, or All In Twin.

What you receive

A decision package, not a generic AI report.

The deliverable is built to help a CEO choose the next move with confidence.

  • Current-state workflow map for the selected function
  • AI opportunity scorecard and readiness assessment
  • Top 3–5 practical improvement opportunities
  • Governance, compliance, privacy, and human-review flags
  • Recommended quick wins and implementation roadmap
  • CEO readout deck or memo with clear next-step options
