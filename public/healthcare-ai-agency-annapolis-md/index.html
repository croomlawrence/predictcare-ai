FAQ

Answers for healthcare leaders evaluating AI commercial services.

What is a healthcare AI growth agency?

A healthcare AI growth agency helps healthcare companies apply AI to commercial and operational workflows such as CRM, sales follow-up, content, patient/customer access, reporting, and process improvement. PredictCare.ai specializes in governed workflows with human review, not unmanaged automation.

Can a medical practice use AI without creating HIPAA risk?

Yes, but the safest starting point is to use AI on non-PHI workflows first, such as content planning, CRM cleanup, follow-up templates, operating checklists, reporting, and internal knowledge organization. Workflows involving PHI require appropriate HIPAA, security, vendor, and process review.

What AI workflow should a healthcare practice start with first?

Most practices should start with a workflow that is valuable, repetitive, low-risk, and easy to review: lead or referral follow-up, inquiry routing, approved content repurposing, CRM cleanup, or weekly management reporting.

Is PredictCare.ai a medical provider?

No. PredictCare.ai is a healthcare AI growth and commercial operations agency. It does not provide clinical care, diagnosis, treatment, medical advice, or clinical decision support.

Does PredictCare.ai work only in Annapolis?

No. PredictCare.ai is based in Annapolis and serves healthcare practices and healthcare companies across Maryland and the broader Mid-Atlantic region.
