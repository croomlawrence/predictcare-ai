Harness vs. Single Brain

Use Harness as the category. Use Single Brain as the memory layer.

Single Brain is the governed memory layer: the company’s facts, decisions, source links, people, offers, and operating memory. HealthFounderOS Harness is the larger system around it: workflows, rules, quality checks, approval gates, agents, and custom applications.

Context

Keep the company's special knowledge organized: documents, meetings, strategy, decisions, offers, accounts, and market signals.

Controls

Make the rules clear: claims boundaries, privacy, evidence standards, brand voice, decision rights, and human review.

Execution

Turn knowledge and rules into repeatable work for revenue, content, proposals, investor updates, and operations.
