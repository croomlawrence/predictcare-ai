01
The problem

AI is everywhere in healthcare. Clarity isn’t.

Most teams are already experimenting with ChatGPT, Claude, CRMs, automations, meeting summaries, and content tools. That is not an AI strategy.

Scattered tools create speed without alignment.

Healthcare teams need AI systems that connect to real workflows: growth, intake, referrals, CRM, compliance review, research, reporting, and executive decisions.

  • Unclear ownership
  • Duplicate work and fragmented memory
  • Inconsistent follow-up and handoffs

Healthcare adoption creates resistance.

Compliance concerns, staff skepticism, workflow disruption, leadership uncertainty, and unclear guardrails can slow AI adoption even when the opportunity is obvious.

  • What should AI do?
  • What should humans approve?
  • What should we avoid automating?
02
The operating model

Start with one workflow. Govern it. Prove value. Scale what works.

I use the HealthFounderOS transformation framework as our practical operating model: don’t try to transform the whole company at once. Build a governed workflow at the edge of the business, prove it, and expand with discipline.

01 · Discover

Find the first workflow

Map where AI can create near-term growth, efficiency, or decision-quality leverage — and where risk requires restraint.

02 · Govern

Set the rules

Define what AI can draft, route, summarize, recommend, or automate — and what requires human approval.

03 · Build

Deploy a usable system

Connect the workflow to existing tools where possible: CRM, email, docs, meetings, dashboards, content, and reporting.

04 · Improve

Scale what works

Measure adoption, performance, and resistance. Expand only when the workflow proves value and the team trusts it.
