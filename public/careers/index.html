The thesis

Capabilities have risen. Expectations have risen.

The best specialists will graduate into forward-deployed operators who understand business strategy, customer reality, AI systems, and commercial execution.

The rise in FDEs also creates the need for FDMs — Forward Deployed Marketers.

Our take: the Forward Deployed Marketer needs to work side by side with the Forward Deployed Engineer. The FDM grounds the build in business and marketing strategy. The FDE grounds the strategy in technical reality and brings implementation ideas back to the team. Together, they create better idea babies.

In our Single Brain pilots, the FDM is the well-rounded marketer who can speak to both business and marketing strategy — the person who just seems to get it. We are extending the same model into Forward Deployed Sales so pipeline, follow-up, service, and revenue cadence work from the same operating memory.

Open roles
