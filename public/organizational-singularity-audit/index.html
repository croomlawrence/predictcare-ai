The L0-L5 readiness scorecard

The goal is not to call everything “AI-ready.” The goal is to know what maturity level each workflow has earned.

LevelPlain-English meaningWhat it looks like in a healthcare companyRecommended next step
L0
The Muddle		Work is ambiguous and mostly in people's heads.No clear owner, no agreed metric, ad hoc Slack/email follow-up.Make the workflow visible: define purpose, owner, inputs, outputs, and risk boundaries.
L1
Measurable		The team has a scoreboard.Pipeline, cycle time, follow-up rate, content cadence, or support backlog is tracked.Create baseline metrics and a weekly review rhythm.
L2
Repeatable		The work has a playbook.SOPs, templates, checklists, approval rules, and handoff definitions exist.Standardize the best version of the workflow before adding automation.
L3
Automated		Agents handle primitive tasks with review.Drafts, summaries, routing, CRM updates, content repurposing, and reminders run semi-automatically.Add human-in-the-loop review, logs, and exception handling.
L4
Industrialized		The company buys verified outcomes, not effort.Reports, follow-ups, dashboards, and deliverables are produced reliably on cadence with QA.Shift governance toward outcome metrics and verified service levels.
L5
Solved		The workflow becomes a boring utility.The task runs invisibly, safely, and cheaply enough that nobody worries about it.Maintain, audit, and reinvest freed capacity into the next bottleneck.
