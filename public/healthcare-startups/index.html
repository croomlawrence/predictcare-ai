Guardrails

Designed for healthcare constraints.

HealthFounderOS supports commercial operations, customer experience, marketing/sales enablement, workflow design, governance, and executive alignment. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, clinical decision support, legal advice, or regulatory advice.

  • No PHI required for the first diagnostic or public-content workflows.
  • Claims, privacy, and clinical boundaries are made explicit before implementation.
  • AI drafts and recommends; named humans approve, decide, and remain accountable.
