# Custom AI Build BioPharma Commercial Strategy Prompt Pack

**Prototype demo:** HealthFounderOS by PredictCare.AI  
**Buyer persona:** BioPharma CEO  
**Use case:** Convert a pharma strategy deck into a governed Commercial Strategy Plan integrating marketing, sales alignment, Direct-to-Consumer (DTC), and Direct-to-Physician (DTP) activation.  
**Important guardrail:** Do not paste PHI, patient-level data, non-public trial data, confidential legal advice, or unreleased regulated claims into public or unmanaged AI tools.

---

## Primary framework spine

This demo uses exactly **three primary frameworks**. Everything else is evidence, technique, or commentary.

1. **ExO 3.0 = the destination**  
   MTP + DRIVE, the intelligence engine, + SHAPE, the organizational form.

2. **Intelligence Stack = the new operating system**  
   Six cognitive layers plus a GOVERN/ASSURE control plane. John Boyd’s OODA loop scaled into organizational architecture.

3. **REWRITE = the playbook**  
   Six sequenced steps from current state to ExO 3.0.

---

## How to use this pack

1. Start with the deck audit prompt.
2. Convert the deck into a CEO-facing commercial strategy thesis.
3. Align marketing, sales, DTC, and DTP activation.
4. Map claims, evidence, approvals, and launch-phase boundaries.
5. Use the Intelligence Stack to define the operating system.
6. Use REWRITE to sequence the transition from current state to ExO 3.0.
7. Export the final Commercial Strategy Plan as a PDF for executive review.

## Prompt 1 — BioPharma Commercial Deck Audit

```text
You are a healthcare commercial strategy operator auditing a BioPharma commercial strategy deck for a CEO.

Review the deck content below and produce a concise executive audit with:
1. Core commercial thesis
2. Target audiences and stakeholder map
3. Marketing strategy assumptions
4. Sales / field alignment assumptions
5. Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) activation opportunities and constraints
6. Direct-to-Physician (DTP) activation opportunities and constraints
7. Evidence base and missing proof
8. Message hierarchy
9. Launch-stage readiness
10. Compliance / MLR / legal review flags
11. Recommended CEO decisions and next-best actions

Rules:
- Do not invent data.
- Separate facts from assumptions.
- Flag claims that require medical, legal, regulatory, compliance, or MLR review.
- Respect pre-launch vs legally approved commercial launch boundaries.
- Keep the tone respectful and CEO-ready.

Deck content:
[PASTE EXTRACTED DECK TEXT HERE]
```

## Prompt 3 — Intelligence Stack Operating System

```text
Design the Intelligence Stack for this commercial strategy.

Use six cognitive layers plus a GOVERN/ASSURE control plane. Treat John Boyd's OODA loop as the operating logic scaled into organizational architecture.

For each layer, define:
- Purpose
- Source inputs
- AI-assisted work
- Human decision rights
- Outputs
- Risks / controls

Include layers for:
1. Sense market and stakeholder signals
2. Understand / interpret evidence and context
3. Orient strategy and priorities
4. Decide next-best commercial actions
5. Act through marketing, sales, DTC, and DTP workflows
6. Learn from outcomes and feedback
7. GOVERN/ASSURE control plane across claims, privacy, compliance, MLR, legal, and executive decision rights
```

## Prompt 4 — REWRITE Playbook

```text
Create a REWRITE transition plan from current-state deck strategy to ExO 3.0 commercial operating model.

- Current-state friction it addresses
- Work to perform
```

## Prompt 5 — Marketing + Sales Alignment

```text
Convert the strategy into an aligned marketing and sales operating plan.

Include:
- Shared commercial thesis
- Audience segments
- Message hierarchy
- Sales enablement needs
- Field feedback loop
- CRM / follow-up workflow
- Content governance
- Decision rights between marketing, sales, medical, legal, and CEO
- Metrics that show alignment is improving

Flag any place where disconnected sales, marketing silos, or lost organizational memory could cause drift.
```

## Prompt 6 — DTC + DTP Activation Map

```text
Create a DTC and DTP activation map for the commercial strategy.

For each path, define:
- Audience objective
- Allowed education / messaging boundaries
- Evidence needed
- Funnel or engagement workflow
- Hand-off to sales/field/medical where appropriate
- Approval requirements
- Risks and safer alternatives
- Metrics

Do not make patient targeting, medical advice, clinical decision support, or unapproved claim recommendations.
```

## Prompt 7 — Claims & Evidence Matrix

```text
Build a claims and evidence matrix from the deck.

Columns:
1. Proposed claim or message
2. Intended audience: CEO / HCP / Patient / Payer / Field / Internal
3. Supporting evidence cited in the deck
4. Evidence gaps
5. Risk level: Low / Medium / High
6. Required reviewer: Medical / Legal / Regulatory / Compliance / MLR / Commercial
7. Safer alternative wording
8. Decision needed

Do not approve claims. Flag review needs only.
```
