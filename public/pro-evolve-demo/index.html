02 / Strategy Thesis

Find the Commercial Strategy Plan hiding inside the deck.

The engine turns fragmented slides into a single BioPharma commercial thesis: market problem, stakeholder need, DTC/DTP role, sales motion, differentiated POV, what must be true, and CEO decision required.

03 / Stakeholder Map

Align every stakeholder without forcing one message on everyone.

Custom AI Build should help the BioPharma CEO generate audience-specific versions of the strategy without creating disconnected sales, marketing silos, lost memory, or unapproved claims.

Executive team

Needs commercial choices, tradeoffs, resource implications, decision rights, and accountable owners.

Medical / MLR

Needs evidence boundaries, substantiation, and safer alternatives.

Sales / field

Needs approved talk tracks, objection handling, CRM follow-up workflow, and feedback loops to marketing/medical.

Payer / access

Needs evidence expectations, access narrative, economic logic, and coverage pathway where appropriate.

04 / Claims & Evidence

Separate strategic language from approved claims.

The dashboard should never imply AI approves claims. It flags claims, evidence gaps, risk level, and required human review.

05 / Governed Workflow

Turn the plan into an operating system, not another deck.

This is the Custom AI Build “aha”: the deck becomes a repeatable commercial operating workflow with source materials, owners, approval gates, outputs, prompts, DTC/DTP paths, sales feedback, and a weekly learning loop.

06 / Implementation Plan

REWRITE the current state into ExO 3.0.

The dashboard guides BioPharma CEOs through a pragmatic REWRITE sequence that moves the current commercial strategy toward ExO 3.0 without pretending the whole organization changes overnight.

Audit deck, current strategy, marketing/sales alignment, DTC/DTP gaps, plan drift, claims risk, and stakeholder gaps.

Design

Define ExO 3.0 destination, Intelligence Stack, prompt workflow, source materials, decision rights, and plan template.

Pilot

Measure clarity, cycle time, review issues, and adoption before expansion.
