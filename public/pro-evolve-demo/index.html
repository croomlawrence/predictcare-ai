02 / Strategy Thesis

Find the one best-practice plan hiding inside the deck.

The engine turns fragmented slides into a single strategy thesis: market problem, stakeholder need, differentiated POV, what must be true, and CEO decision required.

03 / Stakeholder Map

Stop forcing every stakeholder through the same message.

Pro Evolve should help the client generate audience-specific versions of the strategy without creating disconnected sales, marketing silos, or unapproved claims.

Executive team

Needs decision options, tradeoffs, budget implications, and accountable owners.

Medical / MLR

Needs evidence boundaries, substantiation, and safer alternatives.

Sales / field

Needs approved talk tracks, objection handling, and CRM follow-up workflow.

Payer / access

Needs economic logic, coverage pathway, evidence expectations, and access narrative.

04 / Claims & Evidence

Separate strategic language from approved claims.

The dashboard should never imply AI approves claims. It flags claims, evidence gaps, risk level, and required human review.

05 / Governed Workflow

Turn the plan into an operating system, not another deck.

This is the Pro Evolve “aha”: the deck becomes a repeatable workflow with source materials, owners, approval gates, outputs, prompts, and a weekly learning loop.

06 / 90-Day Plan

Diagnose → Design → Pilot → Prove → Scale.

The dashboard guides clients through a realistic 90-day implementation plan that small healthcare/pharma teams can execute.

Diagnose

Audit deck, AI usage, plan drift, claims risk, and stakeholder gaps.

Design

Define prompt workflow, source materials, decision rights, and plan template.

Pilot

Run the strategy plan through one use case or stakeholder path.

Prove / Scale

Measure clarity, cycle time, review issues, and adoption before expansion.
