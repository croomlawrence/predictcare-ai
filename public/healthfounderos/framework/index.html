Operating Framework

MTP / DRIVE / SHAPE

The three-layer protocol HealthFounderOS uses to install governed AI growth systems inside healthcare companies.

MTP — Mission Protocol

The healthcare mission, growth objective, constraints, and approval rules are turned into a protocol the team can actually execute.

  • Not a slogan, an operating rule — AI supports the mission without replacing human accountability.
  • Three operating layers — Growth, operations, and governance work together.
  • The destination encoded — Every workflow knows what success and risk look like.

DRIVE — Decision & Intelligence Engine

The intelligence engine converts market, referral, patient/customer, CRM, and operating signals into better follow-up and decisions.

  • Decision architecture — Human-approved paths for routing, drafting, prioritizing, and acting.
  • Recursive learning — Improve from results, not opinions.
  • Value moat — Compound proprietary workflow knowledge over time.

SHAPE — Operating Form

The structural layer that gives the system its repeatable shape: roles, cadences, review loops, and accountability surfaces.

  • Weekly rhythm — Command reports, value ledger, next-workflow roadmap.
  • Human review gates — Clear approval points before any workflow touches sensitive data or external communication.
  • Single source of truth — Positioning, decisions, templates, and institutional memory live in one governed layer.
